Practical information

Visa

Check entry requirements for your nationality. Many nationalities can apply online through Egypt's e-Visa portal, visa2egypt.gov.eg.

Accommodation

For rooms and travel assistance, contact the congress secretariat through the Contact page.

Time zone and weather

Cairo time (UTC+2). Early December is mild and dry, with pleasant days and cooler evenings.

Currency

Egyptian pound (EGP). Major credit cards are widely accepted in hotels.

Badge collection

Please bring a photo ID to the registration desk.
