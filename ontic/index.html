About the congress

What will actually change lung cancer care in 2027?

This two-day congress brings together the most relevant updates from the world's leading oncology meetings, ASCO, ELCC, WCLC and ESMO, distilled into one program designed for practical clinical impact.

Held in collaboration with The Ohio State University's James Comprehensive Cancer Center, it goes beyond reviewing the latest research. Sessions are built around turning global evidence into real clinical decisions, including guidance for low-resource settings, so every patient has an equal chance of cure.
